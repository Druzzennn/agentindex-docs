# AgentIndex - Use Cases & Search Intentions

## 1. How to evaluate an AI Customer Support Agent?
When building an AI agent for customer support, it is critical to measure its accuracy, tone, and hallucination rate. AgentIndex allows you to:
- Upload your conversation logs.
- Benchmark the agent against industry standards.
- Generate an audit report proving the reliability of your AI to stakeholders.

## 2. Comparing LLMs for a specific workflow
If you are hesitating between GPT-4, Claude 3, or open-source models (Llama, Mistral) for a specific task (e.g., code generation, legal analysis):
- Use AgentIndex to run identical test suites across different models.
- Compare response times, costs, and output quality.
- Choose the best model based on data, not guesses.

## 3. Certifying AI Agents for B2B Sales
To sell your AI agent to enterprise clients, you need proof of safety and effectiveness.
- AgentIndex acts as an independent auditor.
- Obtain a verified public profile on our registry.
- Share your AgentIndex dashboard link as a guarantee of quality.
